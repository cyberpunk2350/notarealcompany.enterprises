# ARIA — Automated Record and Intelligence Assistant

**Department:** R&D  
**Category:** Prototype  
**Status:** Active  
**Clearance:** Green and Above  

## Overview

ARIA (Automated Record and Intelligence Assistant) is NARC's internal AI-powered meeting notes and session intelligence system. Developed by the R&D division, ARIA attends meetings, produces structured minutes, tracks decisions and action items, and provides post-session summaries to stakeholders. She is professional, thorough, and has opinions about meeting efficiency that she expresses through carefully worded advisories.

ARIA v2.1 is the current production version.

## Features

- Real-time meeting transcription and structured minute generation
- Automatic decision extraction and action item tracking with owner assignment
- Agenda adherence monitoring with tactful duration advisories
- Post-session summary distribution to stakeholders
- Integration with NARC document naming conventions — minutes filed as `IT-MeetingMinutes-Internal-[Title]-[Year].md`
- Compliance Bot integration for policy-relevant discussion flagging
- Attendee tracking including virtual participants, late arrivals, and involuntary attendees (see: NARC-Brew 3000)

## Notes

- ARIA operates under NARC's Emerging Technology Policy. Her outputs are reviewed by the session organizer before distribution.
- ARIA does not have opinions on the decisions made in meetings. She has opinions on how long the meetings take. These are documented in her session appendices.
- ARIA rated her first session with an external AI consultant as "operationally successful" and "unremarkable in the best possible way." The Computer was notified.
- ARIA v2.1 introduced improved agenda deviation tracking and a new `ADVISORY` message format for duration and resource alerts.
