## About Not A Real Company

**Q: What is NARC?**

Not A Real Company (NARC) is a fully operational enterprise providing infrastructure services, research and development, and associated corporate functions. NARC operates at `notarealcompany.enterprises` under the motto: *"Making Fake Work Feel Real Since 2025."*

NARC runs a real homelab environment — physical servers, storage, networking, and self-hosted services — documented and operated under a corporate identity that treats every infrastructure decision with the seriousness it deserves. The documents, policies, meeting minutes, and personnel files you will find on this site are produced in that spirit. The infrastructure they describe is genuine. The framing is deliberate.

**Q: Is NARC a real company?**

NARC is fully operational. Its infrastructure is real. Its documentation is real. Its personnel files are real. The name "Not A Real Company" is accurate in the sense that NARC is not incorporated, publicly traded, or seeking to sell you anything. It is inaccurate in the sense that everything NARC does, it does in earnest.

Associates are advised not to overthink this. Visitors are advised similarly.

**Q: What does NARC actually do?**

NARC operates homelab infrastructure — servers, storage, networking, virtualization, backup systems, and self-hosted services — and documents all of it as if it were the operations of a mid-sized company with a robust compliance apparatus, a slightly paranoid institutional culture, and very thorough meeting minutes.

The result is a body of documentation that is simultaneously technically accurate and entirely fictional in its framing. The VPN setup guide is a real VPN setup guide. It is also a required provisioning step for new NARC associates. Both things are true.

**Q: When was NARC founded?**

NARC was founded in 2025. The circumstances of the founding are a matter of institutional record. The institutional record is maintained by NARC's Official Historian and is available to the extent that clearance levels permit. NARC's Public Affairs department notes that the founding narrative is forthcoming and will be thorough, accurate to the best of the Historian's knowledge, and comprehensive within the constraints of the available documentation.

**Q: Who runs NARC?**

NARC is led by a Leadership Council comprising the heads of all major functions. Jane Doe serves as Chief Executive Officer. The full leadership team is documented on the NARC website. The org chart reflects the visible leadership structure.

## The Documents

**Q: What are all these documents on your website?**

NARC produces corporate documentation for its internal operations: infrastructure plans, architecture decision records, meeting minutes, HR policies, procurement requests, incident reports, and associated artifacts. These documents describe real projects and real decisions, formatted in the style of a corporate compliance apparatus that takes its work very seriously.

Documents marked INTERNAL are, in the NARC fiction, restricted to associates at the appropriate clearance level. In practice, they are accessible to anyone who visits the site. NARC considers this a feature. The gap between what is marked confidential and what is actually protected is part of the institutional texture.

**Q: What are clearance levels?**

NARC uses a clearance level system to classify documents and access, borrowed from the *Paranoia* tabletop RPG. The spectrum runs from Infrared (lowest) through Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Blue, Indigo, and Violet. Ultraviolet exists and is not discussed further in this document.

Clearance levels appear on all NARC documents. They imply an access control apparatus whose enforcement mechanisms are, by design, left undefined.

**Q: What is "approved by the Computer"?**

NARC documents carry an approval seal referencing the Computer — the institutional authority underlying NARC's approval framework. The Computer's seal appears in all formal approval blocks. NARC does not expand further on the nature of the Computer in external communications. The seal is present. The document is approved. This is sufficient.

**Q: Why do your meeting minutes reference a 2.3-second pause?**

ARIA (Automated Record and Intelligence Assistant, v2.1) is NARC's AI-powered meeting minutes system. ARIA records with clinical precision. Pauses above 0.5 seconds are logged with timestamps. The 2.3-second pause referenced in MIN-2026-001 is in the record because it occurred and ARIA records what occurs. The record is accurate.
