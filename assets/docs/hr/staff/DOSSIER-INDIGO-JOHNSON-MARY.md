### ROLE & RESPONSIBILITIES

The Chief Technology Officer holds accountability for NARC's technology strategy, R&D oversight, and the conditions under which innovation is permitted to occur. Mary Johnson serves as the structural translation layer between the Leadership Council and the Research & Development department — translating R&D output into something the Leadership Council can approve, and translating Leadership Council expectations into constraints R&D will partially acknowledge.

Her formal responsibilities include: technology roadmap ownership, R&D executive sponsorship, cross-departmental systems reliability standards, and the maintenance of what the CTO's office refers to internally as an acceptable equilibrium. Whether the equilibrium is genuinely acceptable is not a question this file is positioned to answer.

The CTO-COO boundary is formally delineated. Johnson sets the strategic conditions under which technology decisions are made. Lee operationalizes them. Neither has formally complained about the other. HR has noted this as unusual and has not investigated further.

### BEHAVIORAL NOTES

The following observations were compiled from quarterly review materials, R&D proposal sign-off records, and meeting transcripts (ARIA, v2.1).

Mary Johnson is calm. This is not a personality description — it is an operational condition. She has not been observed raising her voice. She has not been observed rushing, including during outages that other members of the Leadership Council found sufficiently urgent to raise their voices about. When the situation becomes, in her assessment, real, her speech becomes shorter. Attendees who have witnessed this report that it is more alarming than shouting.

She speaks in precise, minimally sufficient sentences. Examples from the ARIA record include: "That is a method." "I would not recommend that. I will approve it." "Please ensure it fails predictably." These have been filed. They are consistent across multiple review periods.

Her approach to R&D oversight is documented as non-interference with structured exception handling. She does not attempt to eliminate the chaos of R&D. She routes it, contains it, and ensures it does not reach production without the appropriate consent forms. Dr. Sparks's constraints are treated as a starting position. Johnson has noted this. She continues to add constraints.

**Status Light Doctrine:** Mary Johnson holds documented preferences regarding system indicator behavior. Non-blinking indicators communicate system confidence. Blinking indicators communicate unresolved state. Unresolved state is not ready. No formal policy has been issued. Engineering teams have, nonetheless, redesigned dashboards, rewritten firmware, and on at least one occasion convened a working group to discuss the philosophical implications. Compliance is near-total. The working group's findings were submitted to the CTO's office. They were acknowledged. The indicators no longer blink.

She has been observed silently reorganizing colleagues' diagrams during meetings. The colleagues have not commented on this. The diagrams have been improved.
