The Official Historian holds accountability for the compilation, preservation, and presentation of NARC's institutional history. Mortimer Chalk maintains the organizational record across all periods for which documentation exists at Yellow clearance or below, conducts oral history sessions with current staff, cross-references available primary and secondary sources, and produces historical documents suitable for internal and public-facing use.

His formal responsibilities include: institutional archive maintenance, oral history documentation, records request coordination with HR, and the production of historical summaries, timelines, and narratives at authorized clearance levels. He is the only employee whose formal function involves asking other employees what they remember about things that happened.
