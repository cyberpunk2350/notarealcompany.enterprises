### ROLE & RESPONSIBILITIES

The Chief Financial Officer maintains accountability for all budgetary operations, financial planning, regulatory compliance, and the structural fiction that NARC's accounts balance. John Smith manages both the real budget and the imaginary one, a distinction that NARC's operational cadence has made increasingly theoretical.

The CFO's formal responsibilities include: expense approval authority across all departments, procurement oversight, quarterly financial reporting, and the maintenance of what the Finance department internally refers to as the Monopoly Money Reserve — the allocation of aspirational budget figures to projects that have not yet been approved, have been conditionally approved, or have been approved by departments that did not have authority to approve them.

John Smith attends all Leadership Council meetings. His contributions are consistent and reliably cost-related. This has been noted as a feature, not an anomaly.
