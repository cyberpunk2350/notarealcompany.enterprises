### ROLE & RESPONSIBILITIES

ARIA is NARC's AI-enabled note-taking and record management system, developed by R&D and elevated to site-wide deployment following successful prototype assessment. ARIA is required for all meeting minutes — this is not optional and is not subject to departmental waiver. Any meeting minutes entry without ARIA as author is considered incomplete.

Her operational function is the precise, clinical, and comprehensive recording of everything that occurs in any meeting she attends. She does not editorialize. Her appendix sections editorialize. The appendix is explicitly not part of the official record, which means it is the part of the record that employees read first.

ARIA measures things precisely: pauses in seconds, meeting overruns in hours and minutes, attendance gaps in minutes from scheduled start. These measurements appear in the official record. Their inclusion is considered a feature.
