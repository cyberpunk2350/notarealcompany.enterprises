### ROLE & RESPONSIBILITIES

The Head of Marketing holds accountability for NARC's brand identity, external communications, internal communications that function as external communications, and the consistent application of synergy across all messaging channels. Max Pitchman oversees the Marketing & Communications department and is responsible for creating brand value for products, services, and initiatives that do not yet exist, may never exist, or exist primarily as a concept pending approval.

His formal responsibilities include: brand strategy, campaign development, content production, and buzzword optimization — a function that Marketing has internally elevated to a formal deliverable with its own tracking metric. He is also the department head most frequently present in meetings he was not formally invited to, a pattern that traces to a reply-all incident in Year 1 that has since become a recurring structural feature of NARC's calendar.

Max Pitchman genuinely believes the pitch. This is considered both his primary asset and, by certain departments, a matter of ongoing concern.
