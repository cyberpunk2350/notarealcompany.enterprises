### ROLE & RESPONSIBILITIES

The HR Director holds accountability for all people-related processes at NARC, including onboarding, offboarding, compliance, morale management, and the distribution of imaginary PTO. Linda Park chairs the Human Resources department and serves as the institutional custodian of every form that has ever been filed, every policy that has ever been acknowledged, and every mandatory fun event that has ever been scheduled.

Her formal responsibilities include: new associate onboarding, employee handbook maintenance, compliance policy enforcement, performance review administration, and the production of email subject lines that are both highly efficient and subtly alarming. This last function is considered a professional signature and is not formally enumerated in her job description. It has been noted.

Linda Park is the employee most likely to be aware of something before it becomes a problem. Whether she acts on this awareness depends on whether the relevant form has been submitted.

### BEHAVIORAL NOTES

The following observations were compiled from quarterly review materials, onboarding process audits, and interdepartmental correspondence.

Linda Park enforces morale. This is distinct from generating morale, which is Chuck Cheerful's domain, and from measuring morale, which is a shared responsibility that HR and the Happiness Office have agreed in writing not to argue about again. Park's contribution is structural: compliance is achieved, forms are filed, and attendance at mandatory events is tracked.

Her email subject lines have been described by multiple recipients as "efficient," "brisk," and, in one documented case, "somehow both welcoming and final." She considers this a compliment. HR's position is that this is not a subject that requires further discussion.

She distributes imaginary PTO with administrative precision. The imaginary PTO budget has never been exceeded. The real PTO budget is a separate matter handled by Finance.
