### BEHAVIORAL NOTES

The following observations were compiled from quarterly review materials, R&D project documentation, and interdepartmental correspondence.

Dr. Sparks prototypes wildly and documents everything. These two behaviors occur in parallel, which means the documentation is both thorough and occasionally alarming in retrospect. Prior assessors have noted that her project files read as if the outcome was known in advance and the documentation was produced to justify it. She considers this efficient.

Her default justification for any experimental initiative with an unclear risk profile is "for science." This has appeared in purchase request justification fields, project charter rationale sections, and, in one instance, an incident report. HR has noted it. R&D considers it a department motto. The two positions are not in conflict because they are filed in different locations.

She has requested AI consultant secondment on multiple occasions. The requests are on file. A decision is pending at the executive level. This has been the case for some time.
