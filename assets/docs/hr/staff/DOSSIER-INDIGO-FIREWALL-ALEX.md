### ROLE & RESPONSIBILITIES

The Chief Information Security Officer holds accountability for NARC's security posture, incident response, vulnerability management, patch compliance, and the formal documentation of opinions regarding all of the above. Alex Firewall oversees the Cybersecurity department and serves as the institutional authority on matters of risk, threat modeling, packet behavior, and whether any given system has been reviewed by someone qualified to review it.

Formal responsibilities include: security policy enforcement, firewall configuration oversight, penetration testing program management, incident simulation coordination, and the maintenance of what the Cybersecurity department refers to as the Outstanding Review Register — a living document of systems that have been deployed without adequate security review. The NARC-Brew 3000 fleet has occupied the top position on this register since its deployment. This has been noted repeatedly.

Alex Firewall files formal objections. This is not informal behavior — it is a professional practice. The objections are on file. They are always on file.
