### POSITION SUMMARY

The Internal Security Bureaucrat is responsible for the administrative and compliance functions of the Internal Security department. This includes the processing, filing, and maintenance of security-related documentation; coordination of access review records; and the production of reports, notices, and correspondence on behalf of the department.

The role exists in a supporting capacity to the Head of Internal Security. Operational decisions remain with the Head of Internal Security. The Bureaucrat's function is procedural. Documentation will be thorough.

The distinction between the administrative scope of this role and the operational scope of the Head of Internal Security will be formally defined upon appointment and mutual agreement of the relevant parties. HR considers this a standard onboarding deliverable.

### QUALIFICATIONS

Candidates should demonstrate familiarity with internal documentation standards, a consistent approach to compliance administration, and a professional disposition appropriate to the Internal Security environment. Prior experience in a security-adjacent administrative role is preferred. An ability to work in proximity to the Head of Internal Security without incident is considered a baseline requirement.

Clearance level will be assigned commensurate with role scope following security review. The exact level has not been specified in advance. Candidates should be prepared for this to take some time.
