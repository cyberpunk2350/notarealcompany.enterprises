# Chuck Cheerful — Happiness Officer

**Department:** Human Resources (Happiness Division)<br>
**Clearance:** Green<br>
**Status:** Active<br>

---

Chuck Cheerful administers NARC's employee morale function with measurable consistency and a numerical precision that the Happy Packets metric system makes possible. As Happiness Officer, he is responsible for mandatory fun programming, surprise snack distribution (real and imaginary), and the quantitative assessment of organizational wellbeing. Happiness is mandatory. Chuck Cheerful considers this a mission statement, not a constraint. The distinction is important to him. HR has a written position on the distinction. Both are on file.

## Responsibilities

- Designs and executes mandatory fun events; tracks attendance and post-event morale impact scores
- Maintains the Happy Packets metric system and produces quarterly morale reports
- Manages snack reserve allocation across real and imaginary budget categories
- Coordinates morale programming with HR Director within the scope of the inter-departmental written agreement
- Provides morale assessments for cross-departmental initiatives on request and occasionally without request

## On the Record

> "Current morale: 7.4. Up from last quarter. Snacks were a factor. This has been noted positively."
