# Robert Lee — Chief Operating Officer

**Department:** Operations / IT Operations<br>
**Clearance:** Indigo<br>
**Status:** Active<br>

---

Robert Lee ensures that NARC's operational functions run reliably, or at least convincingly so — a distinction the record suggests he takes seriously. As COO, he oversees IT Operations and the ITCRuD sub-division, chairs infrastructure planning, and serves as the organizational authority on processes that are technically sound, thoroughly documented, and unlikely to generate a follow-up memo. He is the person most likely to have already documented the thing you are about to raise.

## Responsibilities

- Oversees IT Operations and the ITCRuD sub-division (servers, storage, virtualization, networking, backups, cluster management)
- Chairs infrastructure planning and operationalizes executive strategic direction
- Maintains the IR-2026-001 infrastructure refresh program and associated project documentation
- Serves as operational escalation point for cross-departmental infrastructure matters
- Coordinates with the CISO on security sign-off requirements for IT infrastructure decisions
