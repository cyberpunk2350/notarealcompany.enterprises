# John Smith — Chief Financial Officer

**Department:** Finance<br>
**Clearance:** Indigo<br>
**Status:** Active<br>

---

John Smith manages NARC's financial operations with a thoroughness that the budget appreciates and the other departments have learned to anticipate. As CFO, he oversees both the real budget and the aspirational one, maintains procurement oversight across all departments, and ensures that every expenditure — approved, pending, or theoretically in flight — has been accounted for. His cost-awareness contributions to executive meetings are consistent, documented, and correct.

## Responsibilities

- Manages NARC's budgetary operations, financial planning, and expense approval authority
- Oversees procurement processes and purchase request sign-off across all departments
- Produces quarterly financial reporting and maintains the Monopoly Money Reserve within acceptable parameters
- Provides cost-impact analysis at the executive level, whether or not the analysis was requested
- Coordinates payroll, benefits, and financial compliance with HR and Legal

## On the Record

> "Does this cost what I think it costs?"
