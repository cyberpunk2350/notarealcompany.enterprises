# Victor Watchful — Head of Internal Security

**Department:** Internal Security<br>
**Clearance:** Indigo<br>
**Status:** Active<br>

---

Victor Watchful heads NARC's Internal Security function with a comprehensive awareness that has never been formally bounded and shows no sign of requiring it. He is responsible for facility access management, internal threat monitoring, personnel security assessments, and the institutional knowledge of what is happening before it has been reported. He is always informed as a precaution. In practice, he is usually already informed. These are related facts.

## Responsibilities

- Oversees facility access control, physical security, and internal threat monitoring across all NARC locations
- Conducts and supports personnel security assessments in coordination with HR
- Maintains Internal Security's incident register at the appropriate access level
- Attends meetings where his presence is considered prudent; the list of such meetings is broader than it may initially appear
- Ensures that all relevant parties are informed of security matters; ensures that Victor Watchful is informed as a precaution
