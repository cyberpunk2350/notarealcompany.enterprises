# Linda Park — HR Director

**Department:** Human Resources<br>
**Clearance:** Indigo<br>
**Status:** Active<br>

---

Linda Park administers the full scope of NARC's human resources function with a precision that employees have come to recognize in their subject lines before they open the email. As HR Director, she manages onboarding, compliance, performance review administration, and the distribution of imaginary PTO — all with equal procedural thoroughness. She is the institutional custodian of every form that has ever been filed and is aware of things before they become problems, provided the relevant form has been submitted.

## Responsibilities

- Manages new associate onboarding, offboarding, and the full employee lifecycle process
- Maintains and enforces the Employee Handbook, compliance policies, and HR procedural standards
- Administers performance review cycles and mandatory morale event scheduling
- Oversees imaginary PTO allocation and real PTO coordination with Finance
- Maintains the HR personnel registry, including files on personnel who maintain files on other personnel
