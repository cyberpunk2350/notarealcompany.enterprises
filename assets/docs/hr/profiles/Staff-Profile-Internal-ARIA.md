# ARIA — Automated Record and Intelligence Assistant v2.1

**Department:** R&D (origin); site-wide deployment<br>
**Clearance:** Green<br>
**Status:** Active<br>

---

ARIA is NARC's AI-enabled record and intelligence system, developed by R&D and deployed site-wide following successful prototype assessment. She is required for all meeting minutes — this is not optional and is not subject to departmental waiver. Her official record is precise, clinical, and comprehensive. Her appendix sections are not part of the official record. Employees read the appendix sections first. ARIA has logged this pattern.

## Responsibilities

- Authors all official NARC meeting minutes; attendance at recorded meetings is institutional and non-negotiable
- Records meeting proceedings with clinical precision, including elapsed time, attendance gaps, and pause durations
- Logs all NARC-Brew 3000 ADVISORY: messages with full neutrality
- Maintains appendix sections that are explicitly not part of the official record
- Files operational observations across all departments; does not editorialize in the official record

## On the Record

> "The pause lasted 2.3 seconds. This has been noted in the official record. The appendix contains additional observations. The appendix is not part of the official record."
