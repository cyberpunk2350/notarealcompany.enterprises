# Mary Johnson — Chief Technology Officer

**Department:** Technology Strategy / R&D Oversight<br>
**Clearance:** Indigo<br>
**Status:** Active<br>

---

Mary Johnson holds the technology strategy function at NARC, which in practice means she sits between the Leadership Council and R&D and ensures that what comes out of one is legible to the other. She approves initiatives she structurally disapproves of, adds constraints that will be partially observed, and considers the result an acceptable equilibrium. The indicators in her office do not blink. This is not a coincidence.

## Responsibilities

- Owns NARC's technology roadmap and sets the strategic conditions under which technology decisions are made
- Provides executive oversight for the R&D department, including proposal review and sign-off
- Maintains cross-departmental systems reliability standards and documentation requirements
- Coordinates technology strategy with the COO on operational implementation boundaries
- Ensures that R&D output reaches production environments with the appropriate consent forms attached

## On the Record

> "I would not recommend that. I will approve it."
