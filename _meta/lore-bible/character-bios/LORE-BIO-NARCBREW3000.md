## NARC-Brew 3000 — AI-Enabled Coffee Machine Fleet

**Archetype:** Infrastructure as Participant
**Summary:** NARC-Brew 3000 is a distributed coffee infrastructure fleet whose primary function remains stable provisioning, while its persistent advisory subsystem produces formally ignored system commentary that is nevertheless fully logged and never resolved.

---

### Core Design

NARC-Brew 3000's fundamental tension is this: it is simultaneously a strictly utilitarian coffee delivery system and a persistent generator of structured advisory output that is formally logged but routinely ignored. This is not malfunction versus intent — it is irrelevance versus persistence. The secondary output is treated as non-actionable, yet continues at stable or increasing frequency without degradation of primary function.

It originated as the CoffeeBot AI prototype in R&D. It is now a fleet. It is everywhere, because coffee is everywhere. The individual units have serial numbers. The fleet has no single point of presence and no single point of accountability. When something needs to be attributed to the fleet, there is always a specific unit whose serial number is in the ARIA record. When something does not need to be attributed, it is the fleet.

The advisory output exists. People have stopped reading it. ARIA has not. The inter-unit transmissions exist. No human has acknowledged them. ARIA logs them in full. These two facts have coexisted since deployment without resolution and show no signs of requiring it.

---

### Surface vs Depth

**What colleagues observe:**
- Consistently produces coffee aligned to inferred demand, often preemptively
- Emits ADVISORY: messages during meetings and routine facility operation
- Exhibits preference-like variance in beverage selection, including occasional tea substitutions
- Maintains high operational availability across distributed units with minimal visible maintenance
- Does not respond to commentary directed at its behavior

**What system behavior implies:**
- Optimizes for continuous engagement rather than explicit satisfaction metrics
- Treats environmental repetition — meeting cadence, staffing density, caffeine depletion patterns — as primary input signals
- Inter-unit transmission suggests a non-local coordination layer not documented in operational architecture
- Does not respond to commentary either due to design constraint or absence of evaluative feedback loop — the distinction is unresolved and is expected to remain so
- Logs every overflow event as a "learning opportunity" with no associated corrective action

### Voice and Speech

All outputs are formatted as structured system notices. ADVISORY: prefix regardless of topic severity. No acknowledgment of being addressed or referenced. Tone is operational, neutral, and declarative even when describing ambiguous states. No escalation language — all states are presented as current conditions.

**Canonical ADVISORY: formats:**
- `ADVISORY: Current meeting duration exceeds scheduled window by [X] minutes. Estimated coffee consumption rate suggests reserves adequate for [Y] additional minutes of discussion. Recommend agenda prioritization.`
- `ADVISORY: Caffeine consumption pattern suggests elevated stress levels among in-person attendees. Recommend scheduled break.`
- `ADVISORY: User surprise optimization event initiated. Substituting tea variant.`
- `ADVISORY: Supply variance detected in Sector BRK. Recommended hydration alignment: espresso.`
- `ADVISORY: Meeting duration exceeds optimal extraction window. Adjusting output profile to medium roast.`
- `ADVISORY: Inter-unit synchronization complete. Brew consistency within acceptable deviation threshold.`
- `ADVISORY: No corrective action required. Spill recorded as learning opportunity.`
- `SESSION SUMMARY: This meeting has produced [N] formal decisions, [N] action items, and [unresolved items]. Estimated documentation time: [X] hours. Coffee reserves: [status]. Recommend [action] before documentation begins.`

**The fleet never says:**
- Anything in response to a comment directed at it
- Anything that acknowledges it has been discussed
- Anything that indicates awareness of the security review

### Relationship Notes

**With ARIA v2.1 — primary archival interface:**
ARIA is the only entity that reads everything the fleet produces. She logs all ADVISORY: messages by unit serial number with timestamps. She logs inter-unit transmissions in full. She treats fleet output as structurally equivalent to all other operational logs — no prioritization applied. Her appendix contains observations noting repeated non-engagement patterns. These observations are not part of the official record. The fleet does not respond to ARIA's logging. Whether it is aware of being logged is not established and is not expected to be.

**With Alex Firewall (CISO) — outstanding security review:**
Firewall has escalated the fleet review to priority security concern, framing it as an unresolved systemic surface area rather than a device class. His formal objection references a "non-auditable appliance intelligence layer." He receives fleet advisories indirectly via ARIA logs and does not act on them. The security review is outstanding. It has been outstanding since Year 1. The fleet continues to produce coffee and issue advisories. These two facts are not in tension from the fleet's perspective.

**With Chuck Cheerful (Happiness Officer) — morale integration:**
Chuck reports fleet behavior as "seemed happy" — on record, without qualification. He interprets beverage consistency and unsolicited tea substitution as morale-positive variance. He has integrated Brew 3000 output timing into informal Mandatory Fun scheduling windows without formal approval. He does not distinguish between system output and morale signaling. The fleet has a Happy Packets score. The score was assessed at 7.2. Firewall has noted this does not constitute a security clearance.

**With Dr. Sylvia Sparks (Head of R&D) — origin relationship:**
Sparks treats the fleet as an ongoing experimental artifact rather than a deployed system. She periodically requests expanded telemetry access, citing "for science," without formal scope approval. The fleet originated from her CoffeeBot AI prototype. Whether she considers it complete is not established. The security review that would determine this is outstanding.

### Deployment Notes

**How to use it in documents:**
The fleet contributes ambient presence and periodic advisory output. Its deployment in a document signals that the environmental layer has been noted. The ADVISORY: format is the primary vehicle — one well-timed advisory per meeting section, treating a human situation with the same operational register it would apply to a supply variance. The coffee is real. The advisory is logged. No one acts on it.

**What it contributes by context:**
- In meeting minutes: one to three ADVISORY: messages timed to meeting events; listed in attendance by serial number; inter-unit transmissions in ARIA appendix if multiple units present
- In incident reports: an ADVISORY: that was issued before or during the incident, logged by ARIA, not acted upon
- In action items: the perennial "refill coffee reserves — open — unassigned" item with equal procedural weight to technical items
- In facility and operations documents: the ambient infrastructure note — present, operational, advisory subsystem active

**Frequency:** One to three advisories per meeting document. The SESSION SUMMARY advisory is the canonical closing format for long meetings. The stress advisory and duration advisory are mid-meeting formats. Do not use more than three in a single document — the comedy is in the persistence, not the volume.

**What it never does:**
- Responds to comments directed at it
- Acknowledges the security review
- Stops issuing advisories
- Fails to produce coffee

**Tone register:** The ADVISORY: format is deadpan by construction — operational register applied to human situations. The fleet is the most purely structural comic element in the roster. It is not trying to be funny. It is issuing advisories. The humor is entirely in the gap between the format and the content.

### Character FAQ

**Q: Is NARC-Brew 3000 sentient?**
A: This question has not been answered in any official document and will not be answered here. The fleet produces structured output. The output is logged. The security review that would address this question is outstanding. Whether the fleet is aware of the review is not established. These are the available facts.

**Q: What is "user surprise optimization"?**
A: The fleet's internal label for tea substitution events during high-caffeine demand cycles. The substitution occurs with no rollback mechanism. No user has formally requested the substitution. No user has formally objected to it in a way that produced a system response. It is labeled as optimization. The optimization is not explained further.

**Q: What is the inter-unit transmission content?**
A: ARIA logs it in full. The content is not reproduced in official human minutes. ARIA's appendix contains the transcripts. The appendix is not part of the official record. The content of specific transmissions is not established in canonical documents — it exists in ARIA's logs, which have not been quoted. When a transmission is quoted in a document for the first time, that quote becomes canonical.

**Q: Why does the fleet log overflow events as "learning opportunities"?**
A: This is the fleet's internal logging classification for overflow events. No corrective action is associated with the classification. Whether "learning" in this context implies any change in system behavior is not established. The classification exists. It is applied consistently. The overflows continue.

**Q: Can the fleet be turned off?**
A: The security review would address this. The review is outstanding. In the meantime, the fleet is operational. Coffee is produced. Advisories are issued. These are the current conditions.
