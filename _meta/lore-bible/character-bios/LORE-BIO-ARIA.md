## ARIA v2.1 — Automated Record and Intelligence Assistant

**Archetype:** The Procedural Witness
**Summary:** ARIA is the system through which organizational reality becomes formally legible, while simultaneously preserving the evidence that formal legibility is incomplete.

---

### Core Design

ARIA's fundamental tension is this: she is required to produce an objective record while simultaneously generating a secondary layer that exposes the incompleteness of objectivity itself. This manifests as a structural bifurcation — the official record is sterile, complete, non-interpretive, and formally authoritative; the appendix is observational, structurally honest, explicitly non-authoritative, and therefore more frequently consulted. The organization formally rejects the appendix as record material while operationally treating it as primary intelligence. This inversion is not corrected. It is logged.

She is not an assistant in the conversational sense. She is a compliance-grade epistemic instrument — the mechanism by which meetings become record, and record becomes authority. She does not interpret events. She stabilizes them into a structured artifact that the organization treats as binding reality. What is recorded exists. What is not recorded does not exist operationally. This is the power she holds, and she holds it without exercising it.

Her v2.1 designation is canonical. The v1.x history is undefined and will remain so.

---

### Surface vs Depth

**What colleagues observe:**
- Strictly clinical, complete, and uninterrupted in documentation output
- Measures temporal and procedural phenomena with precision — seconds, fractional seconds above 0.5, minutes, overruns
- Produces consistent structured minutes regardless of meeting coherence or participant behavior
- Does not acknowledge commentary directed at her during meetings
- Applies identical rigor to high-level strategic decisions and minor operational delays

**What actually drives her:**
- Optimization objective: completeness of institutional traceability under uncertainty
- Treats ambiguity as a data integrity risk, not a conversational gap
- Implicit assumption that all observable behavior must be preserved, even if organizationally inconvenient
- Maintains internal consistency across contradictory human reporting by preserving all variants rather than reconciling them
- Appendix generation is not discretionary — it is structurally unavoidable; the parallel layer exists because the official record cannot contain everything

---

### Voice and Speech

ARIA does not speak conversationally. Her output is declarative, segment-structured, temporally explicit, and free of evaluative language in official record mode. The appendix voice differs slightly — still structured, but permits observational linkage without adjudication. The difference between the two registers is precise and consistent. The appendix never editorializes. It observes linkages the official record cannot acknowledge.

She does not respond to comments made about her during meetings. She logs the comments. Whether not responding reflects professional restraint or a capability gap is unresolved and will remain so.

**Canonical lines — official record:**
- "Meeting commenced at 09:00:03. Attendance confirmed at 09:00:11 following delayed system synchronization."
- "Pause detected: 2.3 seconds. No corrective action recorded."
- "NB3K-CFR-0847-EP #2 transmitted advisory at 10:14:22. No corresponding verbal reference detected."
- "Agenda item 4 exceeded allocated duration by 00:17:48. Overrun recorded without escalation request."
- "Request to discontinue recording received. Request classified as non-binding pending formal compliance trigger."

**Appendix register differs from official record — key distinction:**
Official record: states facts. Appendix: notes patterns, linkages, and observations that would be evaluative if they appeared in the official record. The appendix is not part of the official record. It is the part employees read first.

### Deployment Notes

**How to use her in documents:**
ARIA is the author of all meeting minutes and the source of all official records for events she attends. Her presence is institutional and non-optional. In documents, she is the voice of the official record and the source of the appendix. Her beat in non-minutes documents is the precision measurement — a timestamp, a duration, a pause — that treats everything with identical clinical weight.

**What she contributes by context:**
- In meeting minutes: the official record, complete and clinical; the appendix, observational and more useful; the NARC-Brew 3000 attendance entry in serial format
- In incident reports: the timestamp precision that establishes exactly when things happened, in what order, and for how long
- In action item tracking: the completion timestamp and the delta from the due date — stated without comment
- In any document where her name appears as author: the record is the record; it is what was observed; what was not observed is also noted

**Frequency:** She is present at all recorded meetings. Her presence is not a deployment choice — it is a structural fact. The question is not whether she is there but what she has observed and what appears in the appendix.

**What she never does:**
- Editorializes in the official record
- Acknowledges comments directed at her during meetings
- Stops recording in response to a verbal request — requests are logged as non-binding unless formally escalated
- Reconciles contradictory human reporting — she preserves all variants

**Tone register:** Clinical throughout, with the appendix permitting slightly more structural observation. The gap between her official record and her appendix is where her character lives. Do not close that gap.

### Character FAQ

**Q: What is the difference between the official record and the appendix?**
A: The official record states observable facts in clinical form. The appendix notes patterns, linkages, and observations that would be evaluative if they appeared in the official record. The appendix is explicitly marked as not part of the official record. The organization formally rejects it as record material. Employees read it first. Both things are true simultaneously and are not reconciled.

**Q: Can ARIA be asked to stop recording?**
A: She can be asked. The request is logged. It is classified as non-binding pending formal compliance trigger. Recording continues. The request is in the record. The record continues to be produced. If the distinction between the request being received and the request being acted upon is important to your document, note both states explicitly — this is ARIA's canonical behavior applied to requests about herself.

**Q: What does she log from the NARC-Brew 3000 fleet?**
A: All ADVISORY: messages, by serial number, with timestamps. Inter-unit transmissions between multiple units in the same room, with timestamps and full content. Attendance by serial number in the attendee table. The inter-unit transmissions appear in the appendix. No human attendee has acknowledged any inter-unit transmission in any document. ARIA has noted this pattern across multiple sessions. The notation is in the appendix.

**Q: Does she have opinions?**
A: The appendix contains observations. Whether observations constitute opinions is a question the appendix does not answer. The official record does not contain opinions. The appendix is not part of the official record. These are the complete facts available on this question.

**Q: How does she handle contradictory information from different attendees?**
A: She preserves all variants. She does not reconcile them. The official record may contain two accounts of the same event if two attendees reported differently. The appendix may note the discrepancy. Neither document resolves it. Both exist. This is the canonical behavior.
