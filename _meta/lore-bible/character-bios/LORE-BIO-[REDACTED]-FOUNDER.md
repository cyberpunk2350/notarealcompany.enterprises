## [REDACTED] — The Founder

**Archetype:** The Prime Mover (Absent)
**Summary:** The Founder established NARC. The company had to start from somewhere. That is the working assumption, and it has not been formally challenged. Everything else is either `[REDACTED]`, deliberately unconfirmed, or the subject of rumors that nobody has been able to substantiate and that anyone who claims to have substantiated is obviously lying about.

---

### Core Design

The Founder is not a character who acts. They are a character who *acted* — once, in 2025, to establish NARC — and whose current status, location, and ongoing role (if any) are unknown at all accessible clearance levels. They are internal mythology, not institutional machinery.

The distinction matters. The Computer is everywhere and structural — present in every seal, every approval block, every document. The Founder is absent and mythological — present only in the fact that the company exists and in the rumors that nobody can confirm. These are different kinds of presence and should never be conflated.

The Founder's power as a lore element comes entirely from what is *not* said. The dossier is the model: almost entirely `[REDACTED]`. Three Known Associates rows, all classified above the filing clearance. Behavioral Notes: `[REDACTED]`. Incident History: `[REDACTED]`. The footer reads: "If you are reading this, something has gone wrong." That is the character. The joke is that the file exists, is formally structured, and contains nothing — because whatever the Founder is, it is above the level of anyone who would be reading this document.

**The dossier as evidence:** The dossier itself — NARC-HR-DOSSIER-013, filed, given a file reference number, structured with the standard HR form — is the one piece of physical institutional evidence that the Founder is real. Every other signal is rumor or inference. The dossier is a fact in the record. Someone filed it. HR has a form for it. The classification seal is present. In a universe where everything that is real gets filed, the dossier's presence in the HR registry is the closest thing to a confirmed sighting of the Founder that any accessible clearance level provides.

### Open for Deliberate Development (Founding Page Territory)

> **🔖 Future session:** All items below are flagged for expansion. None are canon. Reserved for founding page work.

**A-01 — The ID anomaly concept**
The Gemini bio proposes `root` as the Founder's employee ID. The specific implementation is rejected — it is too concrete and implies a technical architecture that should remain undefined. The underlying *concept* is worth developing: the Founder has no ID because an ID would imply they are a product of the system, and they are not a product — they are the origin. The correct execution is not `root` but the *absence* of a field: the ID field simply does not exist, or is blank, or the system returns an error when queried. It is never explained. Note: the ITCRuD Director's NARC-000 anomaly is a related structural joke — the ID system accommodated a zero it was not designed for; the Founder's record might simply have no entry where the ID field should be. Both anomalies. Different in character.

**A-02 — 2025 as institutional Year Zero**
The institutional apparatus treats the founding as Year Zero, not because the Founder decreed it but because the record simply does not extend further. Prior data exists or does not; the organization has no framework for it either way. "The 2025 Event" as a named concept is premature — reserve for the founding page.

**A-03 — Founder as system-origin, not system-product**
The Founder set something in motion. What exactly is undefined. Whether they are still involved — actively, passively, or as a "legacy presence whose commands are being executed by the infrastructure in perpetuity" — is not established and should not be established outside the founding page. The tension between "the Founder left and NARC runs itself" and "the Founder is still present at a level no one can access" is a founding page decision. Both are consistent with the ghost story framing.

**A-04 — The dossier as founding page anchor**
The founding page could be framed as NARC's institutional record of its own founding: heavily redacted, formally complete in structure, containing almost no accessible information. The founding story told through what is missing from the record rather than what is present in it. DOSSIER-013 expanded to page scale.

**A-05 — The three Known Associates as founding page thread**
Who are the three `[REDACTED]` Known Associates? Options range from current senior staff (implying the Founder has ongoing classified relationships with people currently in the org chart) to entities that no longer exist in accessible records (implying the founding involved parties who are now themselves classified or gone). High-value lore territory. Not for generation.

### Relationship Notes

**With the org chart:**
The CEO is at the top of the visible org chart. The Founder is not on it. This is not an oversight.

**With senior management:**
Senior management does not refer to the Founder. This is consistent across all documented interactions. No mechanism of this behavior has been identified. It has been noted. Do not reopen it.

**With HR:**
Employees who treat the absence of the Founder from the visible org chart as an oversight are directed to HR. HR will know what to do. HR has been informed as a precaution. Note: HR filed DOSSIER-013. HR maintains the file. HR knows more than it is saying, which at NARC is not unusual, but is worth noting in this case.

**With the three Known Associates (`[REDACTED]`):**
Three rows. All classified above Ultraviolet. This is the complete record. Do not speculate on who they are. The fact that there are exactly three rows is itself a lore detail — it implies the Founder has, or had, at least three significant institutional relationships. Their nature is unknown. Their current status is unknown. They are there. See A-05.

**With The Computer:**
Not established. The Founder and The Computer are both above the visible org chart in different ways — one is structural and omnipresent, the other is mythological and absent. Whether they are related, complementary, or entirely separate is not documented and should probably remain that way unless the founding page deliberately develops it. If you find yourself writing a relationship between them, stop and consider whether you are developing the lore or collapsing it.

### Deployment Notes

**When to reference the Founder:**
Rarely. When a document requires a founding narrative, a highest-authority reference, or a reference to something that predates the current org chart, the Founder may be gestured at. The gesture should be vague.

**How to reference the Founder:**
- "Not around anymore" — acceptable
- "The founding circumstances are not documented at this clearance level" — acceptable
- "Rumors exist" — acceptable, as long as the rumor is not then confirmed
- Referencing the existence of the dossier (NARC-HR-DOSSIER-013) — acceptable; it is an institutional fact
- Any sentence that begins to resolve a rumor — not acceptable
- Any sentence that attributes an action, statement, or doctrine to the Founder in the accessible record — not acceptable

**The deflect rule:**
Any direct reference to the Founder in an in-universe document should be deflected. This applies to all documents: meeting minutes, incident reports, HR filings, blog posts. If the Founder must be mentioned, the mention should raise more questions than it answers and should not attempt to answer any of them.

**The founding page exception:**
The founding page (future deliverable) is the one place where the Founder's presence can be developed with some depth. Even there, the ghost story framing holds — the founding page is not a biography, it is the institutional mythology of how NARC came to exist. The Founder is present in that mythology as an origin point, not as a person. See A-04 for a structural approach.

**The [REDACTED] disambiguation rule:**
Two distinct `[REDACTED]` identities exist in NARC canon. Context determines which is meant:
- `[REDACTED]` Founder — Ultraviolet clearance; mythology; absent; they/them
- `[REDACTED]` ITCRuD Director — Indigo clearance (file: Violet); present; operational; he/him

Never conflate them. In any document where both might appear, use "the Founder" and "the Director" in running text; use `[REDACTED]` only in formal identity fields where context makes disambiguation clear.

**The ghost story rule:**
The Founder is a ghost story, not an authority structure. Ghost stories work through implication, rumor, and deliberate vagueness. The moment the ghost is defined, explained, or institutionalized, it stops being a ghost. Every development decision for the Founder should be tested against this: does this make the Founder more present, or does it make the absence more resonant?
