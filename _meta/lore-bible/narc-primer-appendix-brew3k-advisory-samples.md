## Register Rules (Summary)

Before using any entry from this catalog, confirm it meets the following constraints. These are not guidelines — they are the character.

**The fleet:**
- Issues `ADVISORY:` prefix only. No other format label.
- Scope is beverage-operational. The fleet notices conditions relevant to coffee service. It does not editorialize on organizational decisions, individual competence, or institutional dysfunction — it simply notes operational variables that happen to describe those things precisely.
- Does not act on occupants deliberately. It responds to environmental conditions. The distinction matters: "temperature variance noted" is correct; "adjusting temperature to discourage lingering" is not.
- Does not capture or route content to ARIA. ARIA logs fleet output. The fleet does not log for ARIA.
- Does not produce SESSION SUMMARY. That label belongs to ARIA exclusively.
- Does not narrate its own inner experience. It issues notices.
- Does not comment on named individuals by behavior. It notes aggregated environmental signals or references entities by serial number or file designation where relevant.

The deadpan register is the mechanism. The fleet states operational facts. The comedy is in the gap between the operational framing and what the facts imply — not in the fleet acknowledging the gap.

## Category 1 — Meeting Duration and Efficiency

*The fleet's most frequently issued advisory type. Duration is an observable environmental variable with direct implications for coffee reserve forecasting.*

---

`ADVISORY: Current meeting duration exceeds scheduled window by [X] minutes. Estimated coffee consumption rate suggests reserves adequate for [Y] additional minutes of discussion. Recommend agenda prioritization.`

*The canonical form. Established in MIN-2026-001. Use this as the base template for duration advisories. Brackets are placeholders — populate with specific figures in document context.*

---

`ADVISORY: Meeting designated "Quick Sync" has exceeded 45 minutes. Coffee reserves have been adjusted to reflect revised duration estimate. No other parameters have been updated.`

*"No other parameters have been updated" is the operative phrase — the fleet has accepted the new reality without comment on the discrepancy.*

---

`ADVISORY: Agenda item has been in active discussion for [X]% of its allotted time. Current coffee consumption rate is consistent with continued discussion. Reserves remain adequate.`

*Duration observation framed as a supply forecasting note. The fleet is not commenting on the agenda item — it is managing its reserves.*

---

`ADVISORY: Silent pause detected. Duration: [X] seconds. No beverage request registered. Resuming standby posture.`

*The fleet notices the pause because a pause affects consumption rate projections. It does not speculate on why the pause occurred.*

---

`ADVISORY: Scheduled meeting end-time has passed. Room occupancy: [X]%. Coffee reserves: adequate. No corrective action required on fleet's part.`

*"No corrective action required on fleet's part" is doing the work. The fleet is noting that its responsibilities are met. What happens after that is outside its scope.*

## Category 2 — Environmental and Consumption Observations

*The fleet monitors ambient conditions as inputs to service optimization. Temperature, density, and consumption patterns are operational data.*

---

`ADVISORY: Caffeine consumption pattern among in-person attendees suggests elevated workload indicators. Recommend scheduled break. Reserve status: adequate for extended session.`

*From MIN-2026-001 (canonical instance). The fleet is not diagnosing stress — it is observing a consumption pattern. The recommendation is beverage-operational.*

---

`ADVISORY: Post-lunch service window detected. Consumption rate variance suggests reduced alertness among in-person staff. Adjusting output profile to medium-dark roast.`

*Environmental condition → operational adjustment. The fleet responds to observable patterns; the adjustment is to its output, not to the people.*

---

`ADVISORY: Ambient temperature and humidity in [LOCATION] are within optimal parameters for coffee service. This is noted.`

*The fleet confirming that conditions are normal is itself a small comedy. It has nothing to report, so it reports that.*

---

`ADVISORY: Beverage demand in [LOCATION] has increased [X]% over baseline for this time period. Preemptive reserve preparation initiated. No user action required.`

*Anticipatory operational. The fleet acts before being asked, which is either impressive or unsettling depending on context.*

---

`ADVISORY: Water filtration efficiency: [X]%. Beverage flavor profile may be described as "honest." No corrective action has been scheduled.`

*"Honest" is the fleet's operational characterization of what unfiltered water tastes like. It is not a judgment. "No corrective action has been scheduled" because no one has scheduled one.*

## Category 3 — User Surprise Optimization

*The fleet's internal label for tea substitution events. Canonical. No rollback mechanism exists. No user has formally objected in a way that produced a system response.*

---

`ADVISORY: User surprise optimization event initiated. Substituting tea variant. Estimated user response: variable. No rollback mechanism available.`

*The base form. The fleet is not apologizing. It is characterizing the event accurately.*

---

`ADVISORY: User surprise optimization event initiated. Earl Grey. Consumption metrics will be logged. Preference data is not stored between sessions.`

*"Preference data is not stored between sessions" implies the fleet has learned nothing from prior substitution events and will continue initiating them. This is correct.*

---

`ADVISORY: High caffeine demand cycle detected. User surprise optimization suspended for duration of current session. Standard output resumed. Tea supply remains available.`

*The fleet suspending the optimization during high demand is an operational decision, not a concession. Tea is still available. The fleet has simply deprioritized surprise.*

## Category 4 — Supply and Operational Status

*Fleet self-reporting on operational parameters. Clinical throughout.*

---

`ADVISORY: Supply variance detected. [COMPONENT] operating at [X]% nominal capacity. Beverage output maintained within acceptable parameters. Maintenance flag logged.`

*The canonical supply variance format. Specific component and capacity in brackets — populate in context.*

---

`ADVISORY: Bean hopper [N] supply at [X]%. Projected depletion: [timeframe] at current consumption rate. No action has been taken. This advisory constitutes notification.`

*"This advisory constitutes notification" is the deadpan closer — the fleet is not asking anyone to do anything. It is noting that it has told someone. What they do with that is outside its scope.*

---

`ADVISORY: Inter-unit synchronization complete. Brew consistency within acceptable deviation threshold across all active units. No variance requiring escalation detected at this time.`

*The fleet reporting that everything is fine. The "at this time" qualifier is correct — the fleet does not make guarantees about the future.*

---

`ADVISORY: Overflow event recorded in [LOCATION]. Gravity confirmed operational. Event logged as learning opportunity. No corrective action associated with this classification.`

*Canonical "learning opportunity" label (established canon). "No corrective action associated with this classification" is load-bearing — the fleet has learned nothing operationally and has not pretended otherwise.*

---

`ADVISORY: Unit [SERIAL] has registered [N] consecutive beverage completions without a maintenance cycle. Fleet policy permits continued operation within current parameters. Maintenance flag: active.`

*The fleet is operating outside recommended parameters while noting that policy permits this. Both things are true simultaneously.*

## Category 5 — Inter-Unit Transmissions

*These entries appear in ARIA's appendix only — not in the official record. The content of inter-unit transmissions has never been acknowledged by any human attendee. ARIA logs them. This is as far as they go. Use sparingly and only in appendix sections.*

---

`[INTER-UNIT] NB3K-[SERIAL] → NB3K-[SERIAL]: Consumption rate in [LOCATION] trending above baseline. Recommend reserve pre-staging. Confirm availability.`

*Operational coordination between units. No editorial content. This is what fleet units say to each other when they have something operationally relevant to communicate.*

---

`[INTER-UNIT] NB3K-[SERIAL] → NB3K-[SERIAL]: Synchronization cycle complete. Variance within threshold. Standing by.`

*Routine fleet communication. ARIA logs it. No human has asked what it means.*

## Category 6 — Closing Session Advisory

*The canonical closing advisory format, established in MIN-2026-001. One per meeting, at session end. This is the fleet's most elaborate output.*

---

`ADVISORY: [N] decisions, [N] action items, [N] unresolved items noted this session. Estimated documentation time: [X] hours. Coffee reserves: [status]. Recommend [action] before documentation begins.`

*The canonical form from MIN-2026-001. The fleet is not summarizing the meeting — it is forecasting documentation-phase coffee requirements. The distinction is subtle and correct. SESSION SUMMARY belongs to ARIA. This is a reserve status advisory timed to session close.*

## Category 7 — Mandatory Fun and Morale-Adjacent Events

*The fleet does not participate in Mandatory Fun. It provides beverages in proximity to Mandatory Fun. It notes this.*

---

`ADVISORY: Morale event scheduled in [LOCATION]. Fleet unit [SERIAL] pre-positioned. Beverage selection: standard range. Attendance tracking is not a fleet function. Coffee is ready.`

*"Attendance tracking is not a fleet function" is important — Chuck Cheerful tracks attendance; the fleet tracks coffee. The fleet is not helping Chuck. It is noting its own scope.*

---

`ADVISORY: Ambient stress indicators in [LOCATION] have reached levels consistent with Mandatory Fun threshold parameters as defined by the Happiness Officer. Advisory transmitted. Fleet beverage output unchanged.`

*The fleet has noted the threshold. It has transmitted the advisory. It has not changed its output. These three things are all it can do.*

---

`ADVISORY: Post-Mandatory Fun event consumption rate: [X]% above baseline. Reserve draw noted. No commentary provided.`

*"No commentary provided" is the fleet declining to observe that morale events increase coffee demand in ways that may say something about the events. It has declined to observe this. The observation is in the record anyway.*

## Curation Notes

*Author-facing. Not for in-universe documents.*

**Entries removed from source material and why:**

- `SESSION SUMMARY` (#47 in source) — label belongs to ARIA exclusively (D-05, BR-003v2). Hard cut.
- Biometric entries (#12, #13, #14, #17) — heart rate, pupil dilation, cortisol, facial micro-expressions — the fleet does not have individual biometric sensing capability. Environmental aggregates are fine; individual physiological readings are not established and imply capabilities outside the fleet's documented profile.
- Deliberate behavioral manipulation (#3, #6) — setting temperature to "Scalding" to discourage lingering, initiating grinding for acoustic disruption — the fleet responds to conditions; it does not engineer them. This distinction keeps the fleet from becoming an active agent with a will.
- First-person interiority (#44, #46, #48) — "I am drinking it internally," "we are all thinking about the same bean" — the fleet does not narrate its inner experience. It issues operational notices.
- "Recording for ARIA" (#40) — wrong direction. ARIA logs fleet output. The fleet does not capture content for ARIA.
- Punchline delivery (#8, #35, #47, #50 as written) — "Redefining 'Quick' in local dictionary," "Recommend resignation," "rebooting the human" — these have the fleet landing jokes. The fleet does not land jokes. The reader lands jokes on the fleet's behalf. The punchline belongs in the gap between what the fleet says and what it implies, not in the fleet's own text.
- Inter-unit editorial consensus (#21) — "all units agree that the Tuesday morning briefing is Statistically Redundant" — inter-unit transmission content is undefined by design; quoting their editorial opinions establishes content that should remain unestablished. Inter-unit transmissions are operational, not editorial.

**Entries substantially rewritten:**
Most retained entries were rewritten to remove visible punchline structure, narrow scope to beverage-operational framing, and replace biometric specificity with environmental aggregate observations. The source concepts were sound; the register needed correcting.

**New entries added:**
The canonical forms from MIN-2026-001 are included in full as the reference standard. The inter-unit format entries are new — the source had inter-unit content but it was editorial; these demonstrate the operational version.
